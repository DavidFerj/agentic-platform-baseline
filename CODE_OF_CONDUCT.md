# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in this project must remain professional, constructive, and safe. Contributors are
expected to:

- discuss ideas and code without attacking people;
- welcome different backgrounds, perspectives, and levels of experience;
- give specific, evidence-based feedback;
- protect private, customer, security, and credential data;
- respect review, security, accessibility, and release controls;
- disclose conflicts of interest that could affect a technical decision.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publication of
private or sensitive information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository activity and to project-related public or private
communication.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their verified GitHub
profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and proportionately. Maintainers may
edit or remove contributions, issue a warning, restrict participation, or permanently
remove a participant when necessary to protect the project and its community.

No person may investigate or decide a report in which they are the accused party or have
a material conflict of interest.
